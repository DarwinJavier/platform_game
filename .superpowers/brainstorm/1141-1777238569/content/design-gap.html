What Code Drawing Can't Do

❌
Smooth curves and gradients — Canvas shapes are geometric (ellipses, rects). The flowing illustrated curves in the braids and clothing need vector/image art.
❌
Painted lighting and shading — The reference uses soft colour gradients for skin, hair, and fabric. These can't be replicated with filled flat shapes.
✅
What code CAN do well — Correct proportions, readable colour identity (green top, blue jeans, brown braids), expressive pixel-style eyes, and a clearly recognisable silhouette. For a 16-bit-style game, this is the right target.
